Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the services.

What is included in the AI Anime Video service?

We use advanced AI generation tools to create cinematic, high-quality 2D anime sequences based on your concept. The pricing is per minute of the final generated and edited video.

Do I get the source files for the Logo Design?

Yes! You will receive all necessary high-resolution files, including vector formats (AI/SVG or EPS) so you can scale your logo without losing any quality, along with transparent PNGs.
